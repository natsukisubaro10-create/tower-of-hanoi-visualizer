💡 How it works

hanoi(1, Source, Destination, _) :-
    write('Move disk from '), write(Source),
    write(' to '), write(Destination), nl.

hanoi(N, Source, Destination, Auxiliary) :-
    N > 1,
    N1 is N - 1,
    hanoi(N1, Source, Auxiliary, Destination),
    write('Move disk from '), write(Source),
    write(' to '), write(Destination), nl,
    hanoi(N1, Auxiliary, Destination, Source).
Prolog calculates the ordered moves through recursion. This page requests that move list from the Prolog server and animates each disk in the browser.

Rule: only one disk moves at a time, and a larger disk may never be placed on top of a smaller disk. Minimum moves: 2n − 1.
